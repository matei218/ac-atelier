About me

Some family stories carry on like a thread. My grandfather made traditional sheepskin coats, and the memory of his craft is a source of inspiration for me: patience, care, and things made to last.

I followed my own path through a tailoring course in Romania and a design course in Copenhagen. My scientific background in the food industry meets my love of clothing in careful measurements, thoughtfully drawn patterns, and the pleasure of finding the right shape.

I love vintage cameras, film photography, and the details you notice when you slow down a little. Join me for a coffee and a chat about you, the clothes you love, and what we could create together.
